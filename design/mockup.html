18. A member's courses

Design notes

  • Class first: the app opens on "My classes". Everything else lives inside one class, like Teams.
  • Join by code: the Mayor shares a 6-character code. Students join as Members, and only the Mayor (or anyone with the right permission) can change roles.
  • Roles are customizable: name, color and a list of permission switches. Mayor is locked and always has everything.
  • The + buttons depend on role: Ledger and Schedule only show them to people with the matching permission.
  • Customization: accent color and module toggles per class; theme, start tab and reminders per person.
  • Money: four record types. Payment (a student pays in), Charge students (a per-student deduction, like a quiz fee), Withdrawal (a student takes part of their balance out) and Pay out from fund (the class pays for something with real cash). Balances read "Owes", "Credit" or "Settled".
  • Charging by course: pick a course and the app skips students who don't take it. A preview shows the total before saving, and a whole charge can be undone in one tap.
  • Dashboard numbers: "Cash on hand" is payments minus withdrawals and pay-outs. Charging students doesn't move cash.
