Design notes

  • Navigation: four tabs (Home, Records, Schedule, Members) with one floating + button for adding a record or a deadline.
  • Money: green for payments in, orange for reimbursements out. Balances show "Owes" or "Credit" instead of only a minus sign.
  • Groups: Group 1 and Group 2 are a switch at the top, not separate screens.
  • Roles: the treasurer sees the + button and can edit; other members see the same screens read-only.
