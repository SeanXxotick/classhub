2 · Upcoming in the Schedule tab

Marks saved together show as one entry ("Mon, Nov 23 to Wed, Nov 25 · 5 classes"). Remove asks first, and says that students will see the classes again. Students see the same list without Remove.

3 · What changes

  • Several days at once, and any mix of classes, instead of one day and "whole day or one course".
  • You see the actual meetings you are cancelling, with time and room.
  • Days already marked cannot be marked twice.
  • A warning when attendance was already taken for a meeting.
  • One notification for the whole set, not one per day.
  • Remove asks before classes come back on.
