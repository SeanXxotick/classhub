2 · Today in the Schedule tab (officers who take attendance)

A green "Attendance taken" chip on meetings that have it. A meeting that has started or ended without it shows "Take attendance", which opens the dialog on that meeting. Students do not see either chip.

3 · What the confirmation shows

Saving a new sheet lists who is late, absent and excused, and how many are present. Replacing a saved sheet lists only what changed, and Update stays off until something changes.
