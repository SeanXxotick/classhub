How it works

Announcements | Forum

  • One switch at the top of News. Each side has its own red count of what is new.
  • Announcements: officers post, everyone comments, as now. New: a tag (a course, or General). Tap "Announcements" in the phone to see them.
  • The same tag filter works on both sides, so "Operations Research" shows its announcements and its forum questions.
  • An announcement tagged with a course notifies only the students taking that course.

Asking

  • Any member asks with "Ask a question": a title, optional details and a tag (a course, or General).
  • Ask anonymously: classmates see "Anonymous classmate". Officers who manage News can see who asked, in case of misuse; the switch says so.
  • The asker can edit or delete their own question.

Answering

  • Anyone can answer. Answers always show the name.
  • The asker (or an officer) marks the best answer. It moves to the top in green with "Picked by the asker" or "Picked by an officer", and the question shows "Answered" in the list.

Top helpers

  • Each best answer gives its author 1 point. If the pick moves to another answer, the point moves too.
  • The Forum shows the top 3 this month; "See all" opens the full list, this month or all time.
  • You cannot answer your own question, so you cannot award yourself.
  • Members with no points are not listed. Anyone can hide themselves from the list in Account and still earn points.

Finding

  • Filters: All, Unanswered, Answered, Mine; a tag picker; and search on titles and details.

Officers (switch "View as" to Officer)

  • The ⋮ menu on any question: Close to new answers, Hide from the class, Delete.
  • "Post as announcement": turns a question and its best answer into an announcement.
  • They can see who asked an anonymous question.

Notifications

  • A new question tagged with a course: the students taking that course. Tagged General: the whole class.
  • The asker: when someone answers. The person whose answer is picked as best: when that happens.
  • All can be muted in Account, like the other notifications.
